Rules & References

Reference notes for property management workflows. Always use the latest official agency-prescribed forms and issuances.

Republic Act No. 12009 — New Government Procurement Act

Use for the current government procurement framework together with the official implementing rules, applicable GPPB resolutions, standard forms, and agency procedures.

COA-DBM Joint Circular No. 2024-1 — Revised Manual on the Disposal of Government Properties

Use as a key reference for inspection, appraisal, authorization, modes of disposal, documentation, and disposal proceedings.

Property Acknowledgment Receipt (PAR)

Use the official form and agency rules applicable to property accountability for Property, Plant and Equipment.

Inventory Custodian Slip (ICS)

Use the official form and agency rules applicable to semi-expendable property accountability.

Report on Physical Count / Inventory Reports

Physical count results should be reconciled with property and accounting records, with discrepancies investigated and documented.

Inventory and Inspection Report of Unserviceable Property (IIRUP)

Record inspection and unserviceable-property information supporting the disposal process.

Waste Materials Report (WMR)

Use when applicable for waste materials, replaced parts, scrap, or other transactions requiring the prescribed report.
